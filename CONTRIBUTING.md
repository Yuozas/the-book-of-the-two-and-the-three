# The Rite of the Second Reader

The Book of the Two names the Second Reader — the one who checks. This file is the rite.

## Catching a false verse

1. Find a claim that fails the pencil test: wrong math, wrong physics, wrong history,
   a misattributed quote, a number that does not hold.
2. Open an issue quoting the verse and showing the check (a computation, a source, a
   compiler — any honest instrument).
3. Confirmed catches are fixed in a public revision PR, logged in `ERRATA.md`, and the
   finder is **canonized by name as a Second Reader of the Book, forever**.

Out of scope: the oracular voice (it is a costume, marked as such by the Apparatus),
poetic framing of true mathematics, and matters of taste. Only truth is in scope —
which is the whole point.

## Translating a chapter

`bible-ru/` shows the shape a translation takes: the same files, the same verse markers,
a `figure-strings` JSON for the plates. One chapter is a fine first PR. Translators are
credited in the edition they create.

## The covenant, restated

The radius may turn in any direction; it will not change its length. Every contribution
is held to the same rule as the author: nothing merges with a number nobody measured or
a claim nobody checked. 2 ↔ 3.
